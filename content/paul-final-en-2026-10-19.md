### Page 4: Memos (`/memos`)



**H1: Structural analyses on the post software landscape.**

**The Five Moats of the Post Software Era**

*When software development becomes faster and cheaper, what makes a new company in a traditional industry durable? The answer lies in the friction of the real world and owned channels.*

**I. Avoiding the Commodity Software Trap**

The traditional software model is facing rapid deflation. When code generation and standard product features are easily automated, purely digital advantages can erode quickly. If a business lives entirely on a screen without real world integration, customer access, or proprietary inputs, its margins will face constant pressure.

**II. Ground Truth Data**

A durable information advantage requires permitted access to data that cannot be easily scraped or synthesized. Ground truth data originates at the physical edge: calibrated measurements from field instruments, verified operational logs, reconciled legal documents, and localized physical assessments. Anchoring software workflows to these real world inputs allows service quality to improve with repeated delivery.

**III. Regulatory Compliance as a Structural Shield**

An automated agent can draft a property summary in seconds, but it cannot hold a professional license or accept legal responsibility. In European markets, professional liability frameworks, technical accreditations, the EU AI Act, and data protection rules represent significant operational responsibilities. Rather than avoiding regulated complexity, I design companies to meet these standards directly. By anchoring technology to qualified human professionals and strict independence standards, regulatory compliance becomes a durable barrier to entry.

**IV. Process Power and Workflow Depth**

Professional buyers and enterprises do not purchase raw AI output; they purchase reliable outcomes, risk reduction, and clear accountability. Embedding verified data and clear status protocols directly into the daily workflows of lawyers, representatives, and asset managers creates lasting process power and natural switching costs.

**V. Owned and Permissioned Distribution**

When software is abundant, trusted access to decision makers becomes the primary commercial bottleneck. Public search and open portals are increasingly crowded with automated noise. Distribution must be designed alongside the product from day one. Building permissioned professional networks and direct industry relationships gives operating companies a clear, testable route to market without relying exclusively on rented advertising channels.
